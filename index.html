💡 Why This Real-World Application Demands a Stack:

  • Physical Single Access Point: Plates are enclosed inside a spring-loaded cylinder. You can only enter and leave through the top.
  • LIFO Guarantee: The most recently washed plate placed on top is the first one served.
  • Zero Spillage: Trying to take an arbitrary plate from the bottom would break the stack, which is why array random access $O(1)$ is physically impossible here.
